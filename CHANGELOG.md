# Changelog — By Heart

All notable changes, newest first. Format: Keep a Changelog.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-30

First playable release: the full W7 mechanism group, built and gated in
the sandbox under the Owner's v1.0 jam directive. The UE 5.8 pairing is
the primo follow-up (see docs/BREAKAGE-LOG.md B-5); the shipped core is
the PLANNING's named seam — C++ via the Emscripten-class route, inlined
as wasm.

- **The Correction** (C++20, freestanding wasm32): she speaks the law
  in a teach phase; orders are then issued on a window and answered
  from the heart — a real command/response loop.
- **The correction ladder**: failure trains, corrects, retries —
  window tightening ×3/4, ×1/2 — and fails the ORDER at three rungs.
  Never lockout; the sitting always goes on.
- **The Permission Plug**: plug orders refuse every answer, even the
  right words, until B speaks the trigger phrase and arms them.
- **The culmination package**: each sitting closes with a chain of
  orders at speed; the package completes exactly once.
- **On the beat**: answers inside the first half of the window are a
  distinct, better event; sittings grade DRILLED / CORRECTED / BY
  HEART.
- **Three sittings**: First Lessons (4 pairs, 6.0 s windows), The
  Spoken Law (6 pairs, plug orders, 4.5 s), By Heart (8 pairs,
  near-miss decoys, 3.5 s).
- **Screens**: 18+ gate, title with sitting picker, the brief,
  gameplay, pause, settings (window grace, text size), victory,
  game over.
- **Audio**: generated WebAudio cues answering player input only.
- **Previews**: `docs/previews/level-{1,2,3}-by-heart.mp4`, rendered
  from the shipped core in demo mode.
- **Gates**: 380 native checks (validation + adversarial) on the
  shipped translation unit, settings/record bridge checks,
  performance budget (0.011 ms/step, 16 KB wasm), scripted playtest
  keeping all three sittings by heart.
